Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What is NookNote?

NookNote is a proposed study-planning kit combining a reusable physical planner with a digital companion concept.

Who is it for?

It is designed for students and learners who want a clearer way to organise study tasks.

Does the demo use artificial intelligence?

No live AI model is connected in this prototype. The sample suggestion is generated by simple browser-side rules.

Is the product available to buy?

Not yet. The product and ₹499 price are startup concepts created for this project.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. This website is responsive and adapts to mobile and laptop screens.
